{# ── TOKEN TACHOMETER ────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Modelled on the Lexus LFA's rev counter, and for the same engineering reason it existed: the LFA's V10 revved faster than a physical needle could track, so Toyota went digital. Token burn has the same character — a fan-out spikes from idle to tens of thousands of tok/s between two frames, which a smoothly swept analog needle would simply lie about. Borrowed deliberately: · NON-LINEAR SCALE. The LFA widens its 5–7k rpm spacing so acceleration is legible. Burn spans idle-chat (~10²) to parallel-agents (~10⁴·⁵), so a linear dial would park the needle at zero all day. Ours is log-scaled — see rpmToAngle(). · WHOLE-FACE REDLINE. Past redline the entire dial floods red, not just an arc segment — the LFA's "prime the driver for an upshift" cue. · THE SLIDING DIAL. The LFA's dial physically slides aside for the menu; ours slides to reveal the per-bot breakdown. Needle = raw tok/s (the physical quantity). Centre readout = $/hr (the one that costs money). we picked that split. #}
